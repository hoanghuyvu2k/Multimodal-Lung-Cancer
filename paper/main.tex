\abstract{%
  Predicting response to immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICIs) in non-small
  cell lung cancer (NSCLC) remains challenging, as established biomarkers
  such as PD-L1 expression provide only modest discrimination.
  We extended the Dynamic Attention Multimodal (DyAM) fusion framework, which
  combines CT radiomics, pathology, genomics, PD-L1, and clinical data via
  learned per-modality attention, with two contributions: a competitive
  one-vs-others (OvO) attention mechanism with a smaller parameter count, and a
  natural language processing (NLP) encoding of 13 numeric clinical variables
  using a pretrained sentence transformer.
  In a 247-patient discovery cohort, repeated-seed evaluation (5~seeds~$\times$~10-fold, 21 modality combinations) gave three main results.
  First, fusing sources gave the highest AUCs: four fused sources reached a mean AUC of $0.763$ and, with the NLP encoding, $0.786 \pm 0.017$,
  against $0.720$ for PD-L1~TPS alone.
  Second, replacing numeric clinical labs by the NLP encoding raised the mean AUC in all six comparisons made
  ($+0.014$ to $+0.024$) with both attention mechanisms.
  Third, OvO attention, with about a quarter of the trainable parameters, had the higher mean AUC at the primary benchmark ($0.771$ versus $0.763$)
  and by up to $+0.045$ in other configurations, most consistently when five or more sources were fused, and with the NLP encoding added the
  two mechanisms reached $0.785 \pm 0.022$ (OvO) and $0.786 \pm 0.017$ (cooperative attention) at the primary benchmark.
  For progression-free survival, the association of the risk score grew with the number of fused sources, and OvO had a higher log hazard ratio,
  12-month time-dependent AUC and C-index than cooperative attention in all eight configurations with five to seven sources;
  OvO with the NLP encoding gave the highest log hazard ratio ($0.495$ against $0.416$ for cooperative attention at the labs-replaced primary configuration;
  higher in all 5 seeds).
  Performance on an order-based hold-out partition was lower (0.703 for OvO+NLP and 0.700 for DyAM+NLP).
}
